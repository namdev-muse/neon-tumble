NEON TUMBLE

Roll the cube. Land the matching face on every beacon — then reach the exit before your moves run out.

Levels

How to Play

  • You roll a six-coloured cube across a floating platform, one cell at a time. Every roll tips the cube — the face that ends up underneath is what matters.
  • Each beacon pad has a colour. It lights only when the cube lands on it with the matching face down. The HUD always names the face currently down.
  • Light every beacon, then roll onto the gold exit portal to clear the level. The portal stays locked until all beacons burn.
  • Every roll costs a move from the level's budget — run out and the level fails. Walls block rolls (no move spent), and rolling off the edge drops the cube.
  • Score comes from the clear, the moves you saved and your time. Each clear earns a rank: S (in par moves), A (within par + 2), B (cleared). Clearing a level unlocks the next.

Controls

  • Arrow keys / WASD: roll the cube · R: restart level · P: pause · M: mute
  • Mouse / touch: tap the on-screen D-pad, or swipe on the platform to roll
  • Esc closes a panel · Enter confirms on menus
